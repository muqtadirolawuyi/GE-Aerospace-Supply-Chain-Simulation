## GE Aerospace Explore Manufacturing Engineering Job Simulation
**Platform:** Forage | **Issued by:** GE Aerospace

---

## Overview
Completed GE Aerospace's Explore Manufacturing Engineering Job Simulation on Forage, simulating the role of a member of GE's shop floor supply chain team. The simulation involved working with real engineering data to support turbofan engine disassembly planning and dispositioning non-conforming turbine blades using GE's Digital Thread.

---

## Business Problem
GE Aerospace needed to determine critical requirements for a turbofan disassembly fixture and disposition non-conforming turbine blades found during manufacturing inspection — decisions that directly impact safety, quality, and production continuity.

---

## What I Did

**Task One: Turbofan Disassembly Fixture Planning**
- Developed a procedure to disassemble a GEnx-1B high bypass turbofan engine core
- Reviewed engineering drawings to determine diameters, approximate weights, and proper fixture location
- Applied geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) skills to specify applicability, capacity, and size requirements
- Answered critical requirements questions for the turbofan disassembly fixture

**Task Two: Disposition Non-Conforming Turbine Blades**
- Interpreted engineering data to determine next steps for turbine blades with defects
- Navigated GE's Digital Thread platform to access relevant engineering data
- Reviewed technician notes and engineering data to make disposition decisions
- Completed three Defect Disposition Forms in Excel for each non-conforming turbine blade
- Collaborated cross-functionally with senior staff, machinists, quality engineers, and aerodynamic engineers

- GD&T (Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing)
